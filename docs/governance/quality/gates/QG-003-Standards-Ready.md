# QG-003 — Standards Ready

## Objective

Confirm that a standard is complete, internally consistent, and ready to enter the established approval lifecycle.

## Entry Condition

A standard is prepared for transition from draft to review or approval.

## Criteria and Required Evidence

| Criterion | Pass condition | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Clear intent | The standard states its purpose, scope, and intended users. | Standard sections. |
| Implementable rules | Requirements, allowed values, and exceptions are clear enough to apply consistently. | Reviewed rule statements and examples where needed. |
| Metadata and type conformance | The standard follows the Metadata Standard and Document Type Standard. | Reviewed front matter and document structure. |
| Consistency | The standard does not conflict with released governance artefacts; interactions are identified. | Reviewer assessment and related-document references. |
| Governance path | Required reviewers, approver, and lifecycle transition are identified. | Review and approval record. |

## Outcome

- **Pass:** the standard may proceed through review and approval.
- **Conditional pass:** a documented, non-material clarification remains with an assigned owner.
- **Fail:** the standard is incomplete, inconsistent, or lacks its required governance evidence.

## Record

Record the result in the standard’s review record and link corrective actions to the affected standard.
