# Quality Policy

TPAD Enterprise Transformation manages quality as an integral part of repository governance.

1. The repository is the authoritative source for governed artefacts, their metadata, relationships, and quality evidence.
2. Artefacts must conform to applicable standards before they are presented for approval or release.
3. Quality decisions must be based on verifiable evidence and retained with the work to which they apply.
4. Quality gates must be applied proportionately to repository, architecture, standards, and release readiness.
5. Roles defined by the Document Lifecycle Standard remain accountable for authorship, review, approval, and repository conformance.
6. Non-conformance must be visible, assessed, and resolved or explicitly accepted through the applicable governance process.
7. Quality practices improve through traceable, governed learning; they do not bypass existing ADR, document-lifecycle, or metadata controls.
